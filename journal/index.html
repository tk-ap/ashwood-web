00NOW

What is being built.

Four connected product directions are being developed under ASHWOOD. Each solves a different part of working with increasingly capable AI: understanding context, understanding products, governing authorization, and operating agents responsibly.

Maturity changes quickly. Product pages and dated entries should distinguish live capability, validation work, simulation, and future intent rather than compressing them into one marketing claim.

03CHRONOLOGY

The record stays dated.

Future product profiles will use a consistent event vocabulary so progress can be read as history rather than retroactive storytelling.

DECISION

A meaningful product or architecture choice and its reasoning.

SHIPPED

A verified change that reached an inspectable product surface.

EVIDENCE

A scan, user signal, result, artifact, or other grounding input.

FAILED TEST

A result that disproved an assumption or exposed a gap.

THESIS

A belief being tested, clearly separated from established capability.

Different work. Same person.

The point is not to look finished.

The point is to make the work legible while it is still becoming — what was believed, what was built, what broke, what changed, and what earned the next step.
