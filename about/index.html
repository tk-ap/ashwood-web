ASHWOOD About

Tahlia Ashwood Peart · TK Ashwood

One point of view.
Many forms.

I follow ideas wherever they go. Sometimes that means being in front of the camera. Sometimes it becomes music. Sometimes it becomes a product, a system, or a question I keep building until I understand it better.

The Portfolio is the image-led side of that practice: modeling, fashion, editorial, runway, and creative work centered on presence and collaboration.

The Build Journal is the thinking-in-motion side: an honest record of products, experiments, decisions, reversals, evidence, and the ideas taking shape while I build.

A principle carried through MeOS also shapes how I work more broadly: clarity before volume. I try to move from identity to evidence, from evidence to a hypothesis, from hypothesis to a small experiment, and from a real signal to a decision. The goal is not to manufacture certainty; it is to make complexity understandable enough to act on responsibly.

That means preferring source-verified evidence where possible, practical reality over presentation, and small dignified tests over waiting for a perfect revelation. Whether the work is creative or technical, I am interested in systems that make the next decision clearer and the next version more reliable.

ASHWOOD is the connective tissue—not a claim that every form is the same kind of work, but a place for the point of view behind them to live together.
