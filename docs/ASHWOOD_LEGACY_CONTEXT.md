# ASHWOOD — Family, Cultural, and Legacy Context

## Status
Foundational context for the ASHWOOD identity. Preserve this context when revisiting brand identity, About copy, Builds/Build Journal, product storytelling, visual language, or other follow-up work.

## Name
ASHWOOD is the first half of Tahlia Ashwood Peart’s hyphenated last name. It is therefore a family name before it is a brand name.

## Jamaican lineage
Tahlia is a first-generation American. Both their mother and father were born in Jamaica.

Jamaica was a substantial part of Tahlia’s childhood rather than only an ancestral reference. They spent the majority of childhood summers in Jamaica, including:

- being integrated into the Jamaican school system;
- attending summer camp away in the mountains; and
- working with and for the family business, GBI Logistics.

The official Jamaican national motto, “Out of Many, One People,” is an important cultural reference for ASHWOOD. The ASHWOOD line “One point of view. Many forms.” should not be presented as a translation or rewrite of the national motto. It is a personal echo of a broader inherited framework: multiplicity does not require fragmentation.

## Myrtle Alvira Ashwood
Tahlia’s maternal grandmother, **Myrtle Alvira Ashwood**, is their greatest inspiration, teacher, and cheerleader. She passed away in 2023.

ASHWOOD should be understood partly as a contribution to continuing her legacy, particularly her example of pairing **education and excellence in everything**.

This lineage also gives additional personal context to the use of **ALVIRA** elsewhere in Tahlia’s work and product ecosystem. Do not over-commercialize or flatten that connection; treat it as family and legacy context first.

## Brand implication
ASHWOOD should not be reduced to a conventional personal-brand umbrella. Its underlying logic is:

- one person / point of view expressed through many forms;
- Jamaican and family inheritance without turning heritage into decoration;
- learning as part of making;
- education paired with excellence;
- distinct disciplines allowed to remain distinct;
- continuity across modeling, music, technology, systems, and building.

This context should inform future copy, design decisions, and storytelling even when it is not stated explicitly on every page.
